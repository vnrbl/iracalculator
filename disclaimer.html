Financial & Tax Disclaimer

Please read this disclosure carefully before relying on any calculation result.

Not professional tax or legal advice. The calculators, schedules, projections, and written analyses on IRACALCULATOR (iracalculator.com) are published strictly for educational, informational, and illustrative purposes. They do not constitute formal legal, accounting, tax, investment, or fiduciary financial advice.

1. No fiduciary relationship

Using this website, entering hypothetical account variables, or printing generated reports does not establish a confidential, advisory, or fiduciary relationship between you and IRACALCULATOR.

2. Tax law and individual complexity

The Internal Revenue Code and Treasury Regulations governing retirement distributions (including IRC §§ 401(a)(9), 408, 4974, and Treasury Decision 10001) are exceptionally complex. Individual situations depend on factors that a simplified automated model cannot capture, including:

  • Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) liability.
  • Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT) and Medicare premium surcharges (IRMAA).
  • Itemized deductions, charitable contributions, and phaseouts.
  • State-specific retirement exemptions, local surtaxes, and residency rules.
  • Trust beneficiary qualification rules (conduit vs. accumulation trusts).

3. Projections are hypothetical

Any projected savings or account-growth figures are hypothetical estimates based on mathematical assumptions such as steady investment growth and static tax brackets. Actual market returns fluctuate, and federal tax law is subject to legislative change.

4. Consult a licensed professional

Before executing any taxable distribution, rolling over funds, or taking action that could alter your tax liability, consult a licensed Certified Public Accountant (CPA), Enrolled Agent (EA), Certified Financial Planner (CFP), or an estate planning attorney in your jurisdiction.

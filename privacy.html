Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026 GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & Google AdSense compliant

1. Our core privacy commitment

At IRACALCULATOR ("we", "our", "us"), accessible at iracalculator.online, we put your privacy first. The site is built around client-side computation: we do not collect, transmit, or store any personal financial balances, incomes, or tax records entered into our calculators. All calculations run exclusively in your device's browser memory.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we collect standard technical log data when you visit. This non-identifying information includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address, anonymized where applicable.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Operating system and screen resolution.
  • Referring and exit pages, timestamps, and interaction counts.

3. Cookies and third-party advertising

We use cookies to keep the site working and, where permitted, to serve relevant advertising.

Google AdSense & advertising cookies:
  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

4. California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to:

  • Request disclosure of the categories and pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request deletion of any personal data collected.
  • Direct us not to sell or share personal data. (We do not sell personal information.)

5. European Union (GDPR)

Residents of the European Economic Area have statutory data protection rights, including the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of personal data. Because our calculations run purely client-side, we do not maintain identifiable personal financial records.

6. Contact us about privacy

For questions or data requests, reach us via our Contact page or email privacy@iracalculator.online.

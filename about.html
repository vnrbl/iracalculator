About IRACALCULATOR

Empowering American heirs and beneficiaries with transparent, free, institutional-grade tax modeling tools.

Why we built this

The "Great Wealth Transfer" is the largest intergenerational shift of capital in modern history. Yet, after the SECURE Act and the 2024 IRS final regulations (Treasury Decision 10001), the rules governing inherited retirement assets have become genuinely labyrinthine.

Most Americans who inherit an IRA from a parent or loved one receive little to no guidance from their financial institutions. Custodians typically calculate only the bare-minimum mandatory distribution for the current year — they do not help heirs model multi-year bracket smoothing. The result is that thousands of beneficiaries procrastinate until Year 10 and absorb a "tax bomb" that can wipe out a large slice of the inheritance.

We built IRACALCULATOR to put those multi-year tax models in everyone's hands — free, transparent, and running entirely in your browser.

Our principles

  1. Statutory rigor. Our logic mirrors the Internal Revenue Code (IRC §§ 401(a)(9), 408), SECURE Act 2.0 regulations, and the IRS Publication 590-B life-expectancy tables.
  2. Zero data collection. Every calculation runs client-side. We never transmit, store, or sell your balances or financial inputs.
  3. Institutional neutrality. We are completely independent. We do not sell annuities, insurance products, or high-commission wealth management services.

Editorial independence & review

Our guides and calculators are researched and reviewed by tax analysts and financial educators. We update our brackets and IRS thresholds whenever new cost-of-living adjustments and revenue procedures are announced.

Need tailored help? Our tools are educational models, and real tax planning always involves personal circumstances. We encourage every user to bring our printed reports to their own CPA or fee-only CFP.
